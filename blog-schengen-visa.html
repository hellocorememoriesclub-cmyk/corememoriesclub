Schengen Visa Guide for Indian Festival-Goers

18 Apr 2026 · Core Memories Club

A Schengen visa rejection two weeks before a festival is the single most avoidable travel disaster we see. Here's how we keep our travellers out of that situation.

Documents You'll Need

Valid passport with six months' validity beyond your return date, proof of accommodation, a locked-in flight itinerary, travel insurance meeting the €30,000 minimum coverage requirement, and proof of funds. We prepare a checklist specific to your trip when you book.

Timeline: When To Apply

Apply no earlier than six months and no later than three weeks before travel — but for festival season specifically, we push our travellers to apply the moment dates are confirmed. Appointment slots at Belgium's visa centres fill up fast in festival months.

Common Rejection Reasons

Inconsistent trip details between your flight, hotel, and application, insufficient bank statement history, and unclear ties to India showing intent to return are the top three we see. All three are preventable with the right paperwork, prepared early.

How We Help

Every Belgium package includes visa document review before submission and a dedicated point of contact for questions during processing. It's part of why our visa approval rate stays high season after season.
