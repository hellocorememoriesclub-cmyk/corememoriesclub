The Ultimate Festival Packing Guide

02 Apr 2026 · Core Memories Club

We've sent enough groups to enough festivals to know exactly what gets left behind — and what everyone wishes they'd packed.

The Non-Negotiables

A dedicated phone power bank, a printed copy of your visa and insurance docs, and a compact rain layer — even for "dry season" destinations. Belgium in July has proven this rule more than once.

Weather-Proofing Your Kit

Layer, don't bulk up. A festival day can swing from 15°C mornings to a warm afternoon to a cold 2am walk back to camp. Pack for all three, not just the photos.

What Our Trip Captains Always Say

Comfortable shoes over cute shoes, always. And a small dry bag for your phone and cash — it comes up more often than you'd think, whether it's Koh Phangan's beach or a sudden Munich downpour.

Ibiza vs Thailand vs Belgium: What Changes

Ibiza calls for lightweight everything and a going-out outfit that survives 4am. Thailand needs sun protection and quick-dry fabric. Belgium's festival season means packing for genuinely unpredictable weather — check our destination-specific lists when you book.
