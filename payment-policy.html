Payment Policy

At Core Memories Club, we aim to make every booking simple, transparent, and secure. This Payment Policy explains our accepted payment methods, booking confirmation process, payment deadlines, and refund procedures for our curated group trips and travel experiences.

1. Accepted Payment Methods

We accept payments through the methods made available during booking, which may include:

  • Credit and debit cards
  • UPI
  • Net banking
  • Direct bank transfer
  • Secure online payment gateways

Available payment options may vary depending on the trip and booking requirements. Any EMI or instalment options will be communicated separately, where applicable.

2. Booking Confirmation & Advance Payment

  • A booking is confirmed only after the required advance payment has been successfully received and acknowledged by Core Memories Club.
  • The advance amount varies by trip and will be communicated before booking.
  • Payment of the advance secures your place, subject to availability and booking confirmation.
  • Festival trips and experiences with limited ticket or accommodation availability may require an advance payment to secure third-party reservations.
  • An enquiry or provisional reservation does not guarantee a confirmed booking.

3. Balance Payment & Due Dates

  • The remaining balance must be paid according to the payment schedule communicated at the time of booking.
  • All payments must be completed by the specified due dates before departure.
  • Certain trips may require full payment earlier due to festival ticket deadlines, accommodation commitments, or supplier terms.
  • Failure to complete payment by the due date may result in cancellation of the booking or loss of reserved inventory, subject to the applicable booking terms.

4. Currency, Taxes & Additional Charges

  • Package prices will be displayed in the currency specified for the respective trip.
  • Applicable taxes will be disclosed during the booking process.
  • International transaction fees, bank charges, currency conversion fees, visa fees, resort fees, local taxes, and other third-party charges may apply where relevant.
  • Any additional charges applicable to your booking will be communicated before payment wherever reasonably possible.

5. Payment Security

Online payments are processed through the payment methods and gateways made available by Core Memories Club.

Customers should complete payments only through our official payment links or verified bank details. Please do not share your card PIN, banking password, OTP, or other confidential banking credentials with anyone.

Always retain your payment receipt or transaction confirmation for future reference.

6. Failed or Delayed Payments

If a payment fails, remains pending, or is deducted from your account without booking confirmation, please contact our team with your transaction reference.

A booking is not considered confirmed until the required payment has been verified. If a payment is debited but not successfully received, the resolution or reversal will be subject to the payment provider’s and bank’s processing timelines.

Delayed balance payments may affect the availability and price of flights, accommodation, festival tickets, and other trip components.

7. Cancellation & Refund Policy

All cancellations and refund requests are subject to the applicable Terms & Conditions and the cancellation policy communicated for the specific trip.

Unless a different written policy applies to your booking, the following cancellation schedule applies:

  • 45 or more days before departure: The booking deposit is non-refundable.
  • 30–44 days before departure: 50% of the total trip cost is non-refundable.
  • 0–29 days before departure: 100% of the total trip cost is non-refundable.

The booking deposit and any other amounts already paid will be adjusted against the applicable cancellation charges. Any eligible refund will be calculated accordingly.

Third-party expenses, including festival tickets, accommodation, flights, and other supplier bookings, may be subject to additional non-refundable terms.

Refund Processing Timeline

Any approved refund will be processed within 15 working days from the date of refund approval. The time taken for the amount to reflect in the customer’s account may vary depending on the bank or payment provider.

8. Trip Changes & Unforeseen Circumstances

Changes to itineraries, dates, accommodation, activities, or other trip components may be necessary due to operational requirements, supplier decisions, weather, transport disruptions, or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

Any resulting refunds, credits, substitutions, or additional charges will be handled in accordance with the applicable booking terms and relevant supplier policies.
