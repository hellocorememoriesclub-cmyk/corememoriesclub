05 / BEFORE YOU SAY YES

A few things
you’re wondering.

Ask us anything ↗
Can I join if I’m travelling solo?

Yes. Solo travellers are welcome on our group and festival trips. You’ll join a ready-made crew and have a trip captain along the way.

What’s included in the trip price?

Inclusions vary by trip. Stays, transfers, selected experiences and on-ground support are generally included. Ask for the full itinerary and exclusions before booking.

What about flights and visas?

Flights, visa assistance and travel insurance can be arranged. They are quoted separately unless your selected package explicitly includes them.

How do I book my place?

Send your preferred trip and travel dates on WhatsApp. The team will confirm availability, the final price, payment terms and next steps before you pay.

Can you plan a custom trip?

Yes. Tell the team where you’d like to go, your dates, group size and budget to start planning your itinerary.
