Chasing the Northern Lights: A First-Timer's Guide

30 Apr 2026 · Core Memories Club

The aurora doesn't run on a schedule, but there's a lot you can do to stack the odds in your favour — and dress so you actually enjoy the wait.

Best Months To Go

Late September through March gives you the dark skies you need. We run our Iceland batches November through February specifically, when clear-night odds are highest.

Where We Base You

We keep groups just outside Reykjavik's light pollution but close enough for a quick return if the sky doesn't cooperate on night one — you'll usually get two or three chances across the trip.

What To Actually Wear

Thermal base layer, a proper insulated outer shell, and — this one surprises people — hand warmers for your camera batteries, which drain fast in the cold.

Managing Expectations

It's not always the vivid green of the photos to the naked eye — cameras pick up more than your eyes do. But standing under it, even a faint aurora, tends to be the moment travellers talk about long after the trip.
