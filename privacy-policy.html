Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information We Collect

When you book a trip or contact us, we collect your name, phone number, email, and trip preferences to plan and confirm your travel. Payment details are processed securely by our payment partners and are never stored on our servers.

How We Use It

We use your information to plan itineraries, coordinate bookings with hotels, airlines and visa partners, and to send you trip-related updates. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Third-Party Sharing

Your details are shared only with the vendors directly involved in your trip — hotels, airlines, visa processors and local ground operators — solely to fulfill your booking.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@corememoriesclub.com.
