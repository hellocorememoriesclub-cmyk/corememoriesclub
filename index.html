05 / BEFORE YOU SAY YES

Frequently Asked
Questions

Ask us anything ↗︎
Can I join if I’m travelling solo?

Absolutely! Solo travellers are welcome on our group trips. Join a community of like-minded explorers, make new friends, and experience incredible destinations together.

What’s included in the trip price?

Inclusions vary by trip. Each package clearly outlines the accommodation, transfers, activities, festival tickets, and other services included. Please review the specific itinerary for complete details before booking.

What about flights and visas?

We’ll assist you with flight options and visa guidance to help make your travel planning easier. However, flights and visa applications must be booked or completed by you independently. Any applicable visa fees, flight costs, and travel documentation remain your responsibility unless explicitly included in your package.

How do I book my place?

We offer carefully curated group trips to selected destinations on specific dates throughout the year. Simply explore our upcoming trips, choose your preferred destination and departure date, and contact our team to check availability and receive booking details. Once your booking is confirmed, we’ll guide you through the next steps.

Can you plan a custom trip for me?

Currently, we specialise in curated group trips with pre-planned destinations, fixed travel dates, and thoughtfully designed itineraries. We do not offer customised private trips at this time. Explore our upcoming journeys to find the experience that suits you best.

How do I get updates about upcoming trips?

Follow Core Memories Club on our social media channels or contact our team to stay updated on upcoming destinations, festival trips, announcements, and booking openings.

Can I join a trip with my friends or partner?

Of course! You’re welcome to book with friends, your partner, or as a solo traveller. Our group trips are designed to bring people together and create unforgettable shared experiences.

How can I contact Core Memories Club?

You can reach us through WhatsApp or our official social media channels for trip details, availability, package inclusions, and booking assistance. Our team will be happy to help.
