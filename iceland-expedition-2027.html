Overview

An overland expedition through Iceland’s waterfalls, geothermal landscapes, black-sand coastline and glacier lagoons under the long days of the midnight sun.

Trip itinerary

Follow the journey

Illustrative itinerary sequence, not a geographic map. Final routing remains subject to the confirmed itinerary.

Day 1Arrival in Reykjavik

Land in Reykjavik and settle in, with the evening free to explore Iceland's compact, colourful capital.

Day 2–3The Golden Circle & Geothermal Iceland

Þingvellir National Park, Geysir's erupting hot springs, Gullfoss waterfall, and a soak in a geothermal lagoon.

Day 4–6South Coast & Glacier Lagoon

Black sand beaches at Reynisfjara, Skógafoss and Seljalandsfoss waterfalls, and the icebergs of Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon.

Day 7–9Highlands & volcanic landscapes

Explore highland scenery and glacier views, subject to road access, weather and the confirmed route.

Day 10Reykjavik — Free Day

A free day back in the capital for last-minute exploring, shopping or an optional add-on activity.

Day 11Departure

Check out and transfer to the airport for your onward flight home.
