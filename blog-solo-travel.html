Solo Travel, Group Energy: How Our Trips Work

12 May 2026 · Core Memories Club

More than half the travellers who book with us are travelling alone. By day two, almost none of them are eating dinner by themselves.

Why Solo Travellers Choose Group Trips

Booking solo often means either overpaying for a single room or hoping you meet people along the way. Our trips solve both — shared accommodation built in, and a group that's already there from day one.

How We Build The Group

We cap group sizes so it stays personal, and our trip captains are deliberate about the first day — shared meals, one low-key group activity, no pressure. The rest tends to happen on its own.

Safety & Support On The Ground

A dedicated trip captain travels with every group, plus a local contact at each destination. Solo travellers, especially, tell us this is what made booking alone feel like a easy decision.

Real Stories

We've had travellers land solo in Ibiza and leave with a group chat that's still active a year later. That's the actual product — the trip is just how it starts.
