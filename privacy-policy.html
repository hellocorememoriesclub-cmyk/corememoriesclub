Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

At Core Memories Club, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you enquire about or book a trip, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, travel preferences, booking details, and necessary travel documents.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to manage bookings, coordinate travel arrangements, provide trip updates, respond to enquiries, and improve our services.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share necessary details with hotels, transport providers, festival organisers, payment partners, and other service providers to arrange your trip.

4. Payment Security

Payments are handled through secure payment gateways. We take reasonable measures to protect your personal and payment information.

5. Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools to improve functionality, understand visitor activity, and enhance the browsing experience.

6. Data Protection & Privacy Rights

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for business or legal purposes. You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your information, subject to applicable laws.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to external booking platforms, payment gateways, or social media websites. These services have their own privacy policies.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be published on our website.
